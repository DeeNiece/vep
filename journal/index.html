2. VEPS alert tiers and the risk line
  • T1: Edge Exhaustion (EDGE EXH) confirmed. On 4H it waits for 1 confirming bar. Strongest tier.
  • T2: MA cross after an EDGE EXH. Looks back 3 bars, expires after 8.
  • T3: PC cross, price closes back across the prior close after an EDGE EXH.
  • GO = clean. CAUTION = a warning flag, read the reason.
  • Risk line (v2.41): 1R in pips; move the stop to entry at +1R; trail 1.5R; target N R.
  • Scoring in this journal: first touch of the alert's own target or stop; both in one bar counts as a stop; win = target distance in R; loss = -1R. Giveback rule: after +1R, close if profit falls to 70% of peak.
  • Marks between scans use chart price only. Touches between scans are not checked (a guess).

6. CVD+ five-question checklist (scored each scan)
  • 1. Reversal candle first signal.
  • 2. FB or DBD for a sell, FB or DBU for a buy.
  • 3. CVD below 0 (sell) or above 0 (buy), or a reversal with FB.
  • 4. FB reversal.
  • 5. Profile weakness at the edge (EDGE EXH).
  • Score 4 or more = early bias, discretionary. Zero-line rule: CVD below 0 and no confirmation symbol means no buy; mirrored for sells.

Riding a trend that already started (study, 17 Nov)

Question: when a 4H trend is already running, which M30 entry works better? Test: 10 symbols, 14 Sep to 17 Nov, 15-minute prices. A 4H trend counts as running when price is above (or below) the 9 and 26 average, for at least 6 bars in a row. Stop is 0.75 of an average 4H candle. Target is 2 times the stop. Held up to 24 hours. Spreads not included.

Entry styleTradesWin %Avg R
Any entry in the trend (baseline)20839+0.11
Chase: break of last 8 M30 highs/lows13641+0.14
Pullback: touch M30 20-average and turn15541+0.12

What it says. 1) Chase and pullback are about equal. Neither style beats the baseline in a way I can trust. 2) Trend age matters. Chasing in bars 6 to 11 gave +0.27R. Chasing from bar 12 on gave -0.12R. 3) Extension matters. When price is more than 3 average candles from the 4H slow average, chasing lost (-0.11R) and pullbacks lost (-0.04R). 4) In this window shorts did far better than longs (+0.3R vs -0.1R). That is the market of these weeks, not a rule. 5) Symbols differ a lot (AUDUSD, GBPUSD, USDCAD good; USDJPY, AUDJPY bad). Caution: about 150 trades, many overlapping across correlated pairs. Treat this as a hint, not proof.

Working rule for now. Join a running trend early (4H bars 6 to 11), not late. Skip when price is more than 3 average candles from the 4H slow average. Prefer the pullback when price is already stretched.
