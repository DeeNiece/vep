How to read this

Source: the TradingView alert log (alerts on the 4H chart only) and 5-minute prices from the same feed. Times are Hong Kong. Price data ends Sat 14 Nov 05:50 HK, so alerts from Fri 13 Nov have a short follow-up window and are left out of any horizon they cannot fill.

Entry is the first 5-minute price after the alert. Against first: the largest move against the alert before price reached its best point in favour. Best in favour: the largest move in favour before the opposite alert. Worst against: the largest move against over the whole period. Now / at close: where price is now, or where it was when the opposite alert fired. Typical 4H candle: AUDJPY 33, AUDUSD 20, CADJPY 25, GBPJPY 45, GBPUSD 26, USDCAD 22, USDCHF 22, USDJPY 30 pips.
