How to read this

Source: the TradingView alert log (alerts on the 4H chart only) and 5-minute prices from the same feed. Times are Hong Kong. Price data ends Sat 14 Nov 05:50 HK, so alerts from Fri 13 Nov have a short follow-up window and are left out of any horizon they cannot fill.

Direction only: win means price is in profit at the end of the hold, measured from the first price after the alert. Uniform rule: enter at market when the alert fires, stop at the chosen share of a typical 4H candle for that symbol (the median of this week), target 2R. Alert numbers: the Entry, PTP and PSL the alert sent, assumed filled at the Entry. If a stop and a target are both touched inside one 5-minute bar, the stop counts first. Open means neither was touched before the hold ended, and the R shown is where price stood at the end.

Win rate is wins divided by wins plus losses and ignores open trades. The range under it is a 95% range (Wilson). With fewer than 30 alerts that range is wide, so read it as a first look. Best and worst move are the largest move for and against the alert inside the hold, in pips. Typical 4H candle: AUDJPY 33, AUDUSD 20, CADJPY 25, GBPJPY 45, GBPUSD 26, USDCAD 22, USDCHF 22, USDJPY 30 pips.
